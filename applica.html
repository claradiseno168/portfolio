Applica

App design
FEATURED WORK / APPLICA
Project for: Coder House.
Year: 2024.
Role: Product designer.
Supported OS: Android.

Applica is a mobile app designed to help young adults understand their spending habits, organize their monthly budget and build healthier saving routines.

Problem:

Many young adults struggle to understand where their money goes each month. With multiple payment methods, digital wallets and credit cards, tracking expenses becomes fragmented and stressful.This lack of visibility makes it harder to plan, save and feel in control of personal finances.

Goal:

Design a simple and intuitive mobile app that helps young adults track expenses, organize budgets and create realistic saving goals from one place.

Designing the solution:

I designed a mobile experience that centralizes expense tracking, budget planning and saving goals.The app helps users categorize expenses, understand their spending patterns and make better financial decisions through a clear and accessible interface.

My Process: Design Thinking

Emphatize

I started with surveys and interviews to validate the problem and understand how young adults manage their money.The research showed that financial stress was not only related to income, but also to the lack of simple tools for everyday organization.

  • Of young people in Argentina are concerned about their financial situation.
  • Of young people don't have a budget.
  • Of young people in Argentina don't know how to save.

Define

I created user personas based on research insights, mapping their motivations, frustrations and behaviors.I also analyzed direct and indirect competitors to identify opportunities and define a focused MVP.

Ideate

With the MVP defined, I designed the information architecture, user flows and task flows to create a clear and intuitive experience from onboarding to daily expense management.

Prototyping

I created low and high-fidelity wireframes and developed a UI kit to maintain consistency across the product.The final prototype focused on clarity, accessibility and ease of use.

UI kit & Design System

I created a visual system with reusable components, typography, colors, icons and interface patterns to keep the experience consistent across the app.

Conclusion

This project allowed me to apply a complete UX/UI process, from research and problem definition to high-fidelity prototyping.I strengthened my skills in product thinking, mobile interaction design and visual consistency while creating a solution focused on a real everyday problem.

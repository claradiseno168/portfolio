Case of Study

Netflix
FEATURED WORK / CASE OF STUDY
Project for: Coder House.
Year: 2023.
Role: Product designer.
Supported OS: IOS & Android.

Users value experiences that adapt to their real viewing habits. Being able to mark what they’ve already watched would give them more control over their journey on the platform, helping avoid confusion and improving overall navigation.

Problem:

When content is shared across profiles —whether with a partner, friends, or family— Netflix doesn’t allow users to manually mark an episode, movie, or series as “watched”, which makes it harder to track progress and often leads to confusion. Additionally, in the “Continue Watching” section, the only available action is to remove the content, but not to mark it as completed —creating a pain point for users who want more control over their viewing history.

User validation research:

User feedback on Reddit and app reviews shows a clear need for better control over viewing history. Many users mention frustration with not being able to mark content as “watched” or clear completed titles. Netflix even tested a “Watched it” button internally, according to WIRED, but never launched it—confirming the relevance of this pain point.

Desinging the solution:

I designed a solution that gives users more control over their viewing progress. By adding the option to manually mark content as “watched” and improving actions within the Continue Watching section, the experience becomes clearer and more flexible—especially in shared viewing contexts. These adjustments help streamline navigation and reduce confusion.

Prototyping

I designed low and high-fidelity wireframes and developed a custom UI kit for consistency. Additionally, I created a full brand manual, including color palette, typography system, iconography, and voice and tone guidelines.

Conclusion

This solution enhances user control in shared viewing contexts through simple, intuitive actions for tracking content. Small changes like these can have a meaningful impact on usability and satisfaction, even when developed under limited time and budget and validated through quick A/B user tests.

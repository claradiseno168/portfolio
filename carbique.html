FEATURED WORK / CARBIQUE BOTTLE
Project for: Webflow University.
Year: 2024.
Rol: Product designer.
Supported OS:Responsive design.

This project was an opportunity to combine my UI design and 3D rendering skills. Without focusing on UX research, I aimed to build a visually impactful landing page using Webflow, integrating 3D visuals to enhance product presentation and interaction.

Problem:

Many landing pages for tech products lack visual impact or product realism. They often rely heavily on mockups or flat illustrations. I wanted to break that pattern by making the product the centerpiece, using full 3D renders to bring it to life within a modern, responsive UI.

Goal:

The main goal of this project was to focus entirely on UI design—crafting a clean, elegant, and responsive interface that could support and elevate 3D visuals. This wasn’t a UX-centered project; instead, the priority was on exploring layout, hierarchy, visual systems, and how to integrate product renders into a compelling landing page experience.

Desinging the solution:

The solution was to create a complete design system with defined breakpoints, typography styles, iconography, 3D product visuals, and animations—all working together to deliver a cohesive and visually engaging experience.

Design System

I started by defining my design system—establishing typography, color palette, 3D visuals, and iconography to ensure visual consistency across the entire interface.

Animation / lottie

Fidelitys

Then, I moved on to wireframing my designs in Figma—starting with low fidelity to define structure, progressing through mid fidelity to refine layout, and finally reaching high fidelity to polish visual details and interactions.

Conclusion

This project allowed me to explore the intersection of design and 3D visualization, resulting in a dynamic and visually engaging product showcase built entirely in Webflow.
